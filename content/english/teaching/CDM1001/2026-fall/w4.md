key_takeaways:
  - "Graphic Design principles describe relationships between design elements and perceptual or communicative effects; they are not universal rules."
  - "Typography is shaped by more than the choice between serif and sans-serif."
  - "Visual hierarchy organizes the order in which users discover information rather than merely enlarging important content."
  - "Appropriate information density depends on user knowledge, task, content, and context."
  - "Grid systems organize spatial relationships, rhythm, and proportion; intentional deviation from a grid may also serve a purpose."
  - "A design principle should be examined through its mechanism, conditions, limitations, and application to new cases."
  - "AI-generated visual variations require critical review because intended and unintended variables may change together."
