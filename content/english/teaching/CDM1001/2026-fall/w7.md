key_takeaways:
  - "Service Design includes intangible experiences, support, relationships, and systems."
  - "A service can change through its provider, added experience, intervention timing, or actor-resource connections."
  - "A new service element should support the core activity rather than simply add more technology or features."
  - "Service value should be considered from the perspectives of users, providers, partners, and other affected actors."
  - "Visual service concepts can suggest possibilities, but actual experience, accessibility, feasibility, and sustainability require further evidence."
