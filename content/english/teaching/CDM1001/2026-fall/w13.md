activity_content: "Teams will apply Strategic Thinking to the Value Ideas developed during Week 12. The Target Customer Snapshot and Key Customer Decision Factors will be used as existing inputs rather than repeated.\n- **Research and Compare Competitors**\n  - Select approximately four relevant competitors or alternatives available to the Target Customer.\n  - Research each competitor’s key characteristics, including target customer, main products and design characteristics, brand image, price or market level, notable strengths, and visible communication or customer-experience examples.\n  - Summarize the findings in a simple comparison rather than inferring a complete hidden marketing strategy.\n  - **Output:** Competitor Comparison Map\n- **Evaluate the Strategic Potential of the Value Ideas**\n  - **Customer-Relevant:** Does the idea respond meaningfully to the Target Customer and selected Customer Decision Factors?\n  - **Company-Leveraged:** Does the idea make credible use of the company’s identity, portfolio, and strengths?\n  - **Competitively Advantageous:** Compared with the selected competitors and their key characteristics, does the idea create a stronger reason to choose the company?\n  - Decide whether each idea should be Developed, Combined, Revised, or Removed, and explain the reasoning.\n  **Output:** Value Idea Strategy Comparison\n- **Build a Competitive Marketing Strategy**\n  - Select the strongest Value Idea or combine the strongest elements into one provisional marketing strategy.\n  - Clarify the Target Customer, Key Customer Value, Company Strength, Competitive Advantage, Strategic Role in the Korean Market, Marketing Direction, and Remaining Questions.\n  - The result should guide product, storytelling, content, communication, retail, exhibition, channel, or customer experience consistently without becoming a fully completed campaign.\n  - **Output:** One Provisional Competitive Marketing Strategy"
