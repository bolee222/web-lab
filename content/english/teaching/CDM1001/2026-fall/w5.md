key_takeaways:
  - "Affordance concerns how form cues possible actions, including correct, ambiguous, and misleading interpretations."
  - "Ergonomics concerns how forms, dimensions, contact surfaces, and support structures enable bodily fit, posture, comfort, control, and independent use."
  - "Materiality concerns how visible material and finish cues shape expected touch, temperature, durability, quality, and use context."
  - "Form Semantics concerns how silhouette, curvature, proportion, abstraction, detail, and anthropomorphic cues externalize emotional and symbolic meaning."
  - "Product Design principles are conditional relationships rather than universal rules."
  - "AI-generated product variations require critical review for physical, functional, and material plausibility."
