key_takeaways:
  - "Art and design both involve creativity and the creation of new outcomes."
  - "Design generally begins with a purpose and requires a clear rationale."
  - "Art generally emphasizes expression, interpretation, and personal perception."
  - "A work’s primary orientation should be examined **through its intention and approach** rather than its appearance alone. Later, for your work, ensure that you are in the art domain or the design domain."
  - "These different orientations may also influence how art and design select, use, and interpret technology."
