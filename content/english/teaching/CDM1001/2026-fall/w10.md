activity_content: "This week is organized as a team-based research-planning workshop. Teams will:\n1. synthesize their desk-research cases and clarify what they still need to learn from Korean users or customers;\n1. select one strategic research focus for the Korean market;\n1. define a focused question area and develop two to four research questions;\n1. design the study, including participants, method, protocol, and visual materials; and\n1. pilot and revise the protocol before field execution.\n**Strategic Research Focus**\n- **Product and Design Perception:** How Korean users interpret the products’ forms, materials, functions, quality, distinctiveness, and possible use contexts.\n- **Brand Identity and Cultural Meaning:** How the company’s Finnish design identity, brand personality, authorship, and cultural story are understood in Korea.\n- **Marketing Touchpoints and Future Experience:** How people might encounter, understand, and engage with the brand through websites, content, showrooms, pop-ups, exhibitions, collaborations, or other experiences.\n**Question Focus**\nWithin the selected strategic focus, decide what your team specifically needs to understand. Possible directions include:\n- how people perceive furniture, design, quality, and brand identity;\n- what they consider and trade off when selecting or purchasing furniture;\n- where they discover furniture ideas, products, and brands;\n- who or what influences their preferences and purchase decisions;\n- how lifestyle, living space, budget, and use context shape their choices; or\n- how they respond to selected Studio Mikko Laakkonen products, brand stories, or touchpoints.\nThese are starting points, not a required checklist. Each team should choose a focused and feasible question area.\nThe study may include needs and behaviors, but it should also reveal how people interpret the company and what kinds of strategic opportunities may exist in Korea."
