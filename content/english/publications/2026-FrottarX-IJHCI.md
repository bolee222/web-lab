FrottarX is an effortful visual logging and reflection system designed to deepen engagement with physical exhibits. Through embodied logging interactions and bodily cues that reconnect documentation with later reflection, the system supports more attentive observation, diverse perspectives, and richer reflection than conventional visual logging approaches.
